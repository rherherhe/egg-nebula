About

The sky is for everyone

Egg Nebula began as a quiet place to look up. When everyday pressure felt heavy, the sky made it feel a little smaller. We wanted to hand that same feeling to someone else.

So we explain the night sky the way a friend would: in plain words, with drawings and stories, from where we stand in the Philippines.

You don't need a telescope or a science degree. Start with the sky above your own roof tonight, and we'll explain what you're seeing.

  • Accuracy before awe

    We check our facts and say so when science doesn't know yet. The real sky is astonishing enough.

  • No dumb questions

    Every level of knowledge is welcome here, including none. Nobody is late or behind.

  • Rooted here

    The sky as seen from the Philippines, with the stories Filipinos have told about it.
