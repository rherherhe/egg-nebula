About

You don’t need to know everything to look up.

Maybe you’ve wondered why the Moon changes shape. Maybe you’ve stared at the stars and asked how far away they are. Or maybe you’ve loved space since childhood and want to rediscover that sense of wonder.

Wherever your curiosity begins, there’s a place for you here.

We believe astronomy should be something everyone can enjoy, understand, and connect with. No complicated jargon. No questions too simple. Just a universe full of things worth wondering about.

Our Perspective

From the stars that guide us to the discoveries that shape our future, astronomy offers new ways to understand our world and our place in the universe.

We want more Filipinos to experience that sense of possibility — to see that space is worth exploring, that science belongs to everyone, and that curiosity can take us further than we imagine.

The universe is vast. Our opportunity to understand it starts right here.
